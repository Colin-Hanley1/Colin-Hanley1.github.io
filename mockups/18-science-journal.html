§ I · INTRODUCTION

The author, in brief

— or, who is making the marks

Hi — I'm Colin, a Data Science and Mathematics student at the University of Alabama, drawn to problems where statistics, engineering, and plain curiosity overlap. Healthcare informatics, sports analytics, computer vision, and the spreadsheet no one else wants to open.

I care about the part where numbers start telling a story. Give me a messy dataset and a hard question and I'll come back with a model, a visualization, and a strong opinion about what to do next.

I like clean notebooks, honest error bars, and work that ends up in someone's hands rather than in a drawer. When I'm not at a terminal, I'm reading papers or listening to records.

HL7 Parser & PHI Redactor

Python · Streamlit · Regex · 2nd place, UA Innovate
