Colin Hanley, a Data Science and Mathematics student at the University of Alabama, has a simple bet: if you throw a difficult question and an uncooperative dataset at him, he comes back with a model, a visualization, and a strong opinion about what to do next. His work spans healthcare informatics, sports analytics, computer vision on thermal drone footage, and a genetic algorithm that drafts fantasy teams better than his friends do.
