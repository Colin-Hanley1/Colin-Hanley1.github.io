Projects

05 · seven builds
  • 2026

    College Basketball Analytics ↗

    A college basketball analytics site I built end-to-end: custom adjusted-efficiency ratings, Bayesian player projections backtested against held-out seasons, and an interactive explorer covering ~20,000 D-I player-seasons since 2021.
  • 2026

    Markovian Inning Model ↗

    First-inning run scoring as a 24-state absorbing Markov chain. NRFI probabilities computed analytically via fundamental matrix inversion and empirically via 50,000 Monte Carlo simulations with confidence intervals. Automated daily MLB API pipeline.
  • 2025

    Groundwater Modeling ↗

    Research with Dr. Jia Zhao at the UA Department of Mathematics. A Transformer architecture with Multiresolution Hash Encoding trained on two decades of cleaned US groundwater data. ASSURE grant awardee.
  • 2025

    Poacher Detection in Infrared ↗

    Fine-tuned a custom YOLOv5 detector for real-time identification of human presence in LWIR UAV imagery. Achieved 76% recall on the targeted class through a pseudo-labeled synthetic data pipeline. Published as an academic paper.
  • 2025

    Agent-Based COVID-19 Simulator ↗

    A Mesa-based agent simulation of outbreak dynamics with scenario testing for vaccine strategies, masking policy, and lockdown thresholds. Household and workplace contact structures, age-based risk profiles, dynamic intervention triggers.
  • 2025

    HL7 Parser & PHI Redactor ↗

    Parser and de-identification tool for HL7 medical messages, with a Streamlit interface for analyst review. Second place at UA Innovate.
  • 2024

    Genetic Fantasy Drafter ↗

    A genetic algorithm for fantasy football draft strategy optimization, packaged as a Dash application used live on draft night.
